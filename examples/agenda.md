# Agenda

The club meets to assign the remaining
tasks for the spring planting. Bring your
calendars; most of what follows is dates,
beds, and who owns what.

## Schedule

| Time | Item | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:00 AM | Call to order and introductions | Chair |
| 9:15 AM | Plot assignment sign-ups | Secretary |
| 10:30 AM | Compost delivery deadline | Treasurer |
| 11:45 AM | Tool shed inventory and sign check | Coordinator |

## Before the meeting

- Confirm the compost order with the supplier
  by **Friday**.
- Print the plot map in *two*
  sizes: the shed copy and the gate copy.
- Check the projector for the seed-starting
  slideshow.
